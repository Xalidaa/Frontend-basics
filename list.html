Top 3 songs' description

Billie Jean
Billie Jean is a song by American singer Michael Jackson, released by Epic Records on January 2, 1983, as the second single from his sixth studio album, Thriller (1982). It was written and composed by Jackson and produced by Jackson and Quincy Jones.
Thriller
Thriller is a song recorded by American singer Michael Jackson.
Beat It
Beat It is a song recorded by American singer Michael Jackson for his sixth studio album, Thriller (1982). It was written and composed by Jackson and produced by Jackson and Quincy Jones. It features a guitar solo by Eddie Van Halen.
